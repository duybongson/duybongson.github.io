Privacy Policy — VocabPop

Last updated: 24 November 2026

VocabPop ("the app") is developed by ForgeByte Labs. This policy explains what data the app does and does not handle.

Data collection

The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No account or sign-up is required.
  • No name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers are collected.
  • The app has no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • The app works fully offline. Vocabulary content is bundled inside the app.

Data stored on your device

Your learning progress (words marked as known/unknown/favourite) and your app settings (reminder interval, active hours, selected topics and levels) are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. This data:

  • never leaves your device,
  • is not backed up to any server operated by us,
  • is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data.

Permissions

  • Notifications — used only to show the vocabulary reminders you scheduled.
  • Exact alarms / scheduled reminders — used only to deliver those reminders at the times you chose.
  • Text-to-speech uses your device's built-in speech engine; no audio or text is sent to us.

Children

The app contains general educational vocabulary content and does not collect data from any user, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated and the new version will be linked from the app's store listing before release.
